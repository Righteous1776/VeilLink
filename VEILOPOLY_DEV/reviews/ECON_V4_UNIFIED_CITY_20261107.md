## V4 implementation (NEW SOLO games only)
- **Finite currency and central bank**: 4 players × ¥7,200 initial cash + central treasury ¥160,000 = fixed ¥188,800 currency. Stored as optional `IntegratedBankLedger.reserveCash`; only V4 has the key. The authoritative reducer compares aggregate player cash before/after every accepted move and adjusts treasury by the inverse net delta atomically. Purchases, buildings, tax, chance, passing GO, mortgage, stock purchase/sale, deposits/withdrawals, investment settlement, bank loans and loan payments use the same pool; direct transfers, trades and rent between players net to zero. No shadow money mints. The save loader validates fixed supply, tampered player cash/bank funds must not overwrite the good A/B slot.
- **Liquidity gate**: a discretionary transaction requiring more bank money than exists returns `GameError.bankLiquidity` WITHOUT mutation, surfaced to UI. Bank can never pay more than its finite treasury or exceed its fixed total supply. This is a board-game money pool, not real-money finance, wagering or redeemable currency.
- **Player → player gift**: `Move(.transferCash, player:, seq:, amount:, to:)`, authoritative `.manage` phase, V4 only, recipient valid, nonbankrupt and different, positive bounded amount, funds checked, transaction irrevocable once committed. Existing trade proposal/acceptance and all legacy/P5 network paths unchanged. UI distinguishes immediate cash gift from contractual asset exchange.
- **Real free-house cards**: V4 Chance id13 / Fate id27 replace only the same-ID V3 cash cards. Grants a *physical house* free of cash cost, only on a full unmortgaged color group and even-building rule, consuming Bank.houses inventory. Deterministically chooses the highest construction-cost eligible lot, stable tie break by smallest tile index. If none or housing inventory exhausted, fallback is exactly ¥800 or ¥600 from city treasury; displayed ruling matches effect. V3/legacy saved cards unchanged. Any card owner-driven property choice would require a separate phase/pending entitlement, deferred to avoid breaking snapshot ABI.
- **Stock macro integration**: stock tick now includes a bounded deterministic impulse derived from aggregate player liquidity, existing buildings and wealth concentration; each posted purchase/loan/tax/transfer changes the GameState inputs. `StockMarketEngine.advance(..., economicPressure:)` defaults 0 and is passed nonzero only for V4 ledger, retaining byte/RNG behavior of earlier games. User sees bank balance / supply and macro bp in bank/stock/asset views. Stock orders remain simulated and use the same treasury pool.
- **V4 identity**: `financeRuleVersion=4`, `reserveCash` set, V4 32-card pool and old market algorithm V3 with city-only external macro signal. Existing V3 archived saves retain `financeRuleVersion=3`, nil treasury, original 32-card canonical V3 list, no market impact and old JSON encoding. P5 two-phone configuration STILL CLASSIC; absolutely no silent protocol/schema or room handshake change.
