## Black-box touch sampling caveat

Owner's logs have raw-touch capture enabled. Per exported archive, 88/99 raw taps on the two near-duplicate iPhone7 archives and 192/125 raw taps on two near-duplicate iPhone13Pro archives are recorded. All these raw tap events carry blank accessibility_id in the low-level hit-tested UIKit/SwiftUI view metadata. This is a diagnostic blind spot, **not** proof that every tap missed; raw-tap events must not be counted as failed actions without a reliable per-touch action acknowledgment. Logs also contain a mixture of intentional navigation, keyboards, control actions, and stress automation.
