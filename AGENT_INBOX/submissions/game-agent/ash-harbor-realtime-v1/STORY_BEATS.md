# Ash Harbor / 灰港 — Story Beats V1

All text below is original to this proposal.

## Tone

Rain-soaked, restrained, lonely rather than melodramatic.

The harbor is mostly dark. The player hears people only as short radio fragments. Rescuing them makes the distant harbor gradually feel less empty.

## Setup

**Harbor Control**

> 灰港东侧全部断电。灯塔线还有四组求救信号。风墙两分半后封港。

No long briefing. Player starts moving immediately.

## Rescue 1 — Old Pier Seven

Condition: first successful rescue.

> 旧七码头：看到你了。别靠太快。

After completion:

> 旧七码头：上来了。你的灯在雨里很明显。

Purpose: teach that slowing down is necessary and make the boat’s searchlight feel meaningful.

## Rescue 2 — Warehouse Roof

Before approach:

> 仓库屋顶：灯还亮着，我们在这里。

Completion:

> 仓库屋顶：收到。前面那片水有东西漂着，小心。

Purpose: connect narrative to upcoming debris without turning dialogue into a tutorial popup.

## Rescue 3 — Breakwater

Approach:

> 防波堤：浪太高了……再靠近一点。

Completion:

> 防波堤：好了。别为我们停太久，灯塔那边还有信号。

Purpose: introduce time pressure emotionally rather than through a timer warning alone.

## Rescue 4 — Lighthouse Foot

Approach:

> 灯塔脚下：最后一组信号。

Completion:

> 灯塔脚下：都上来了。继续往前，塔上还能重新送电。

## Ending A — All four rescued

> 灯塔：四组信号都回来了。今晚灰港不是全黑。

Visual: distant lighthouse and several small harbor windows return to warm light.

## Ending B — Reached harbor with survivors left behind

> 灯塔：你到了。还有信号留在风里。

Visual: lighthouse returns, but fewer background lights switch on.

## Ending C — Hull lost

> 港务台：失去你的应答。航迹最后位置已记录。

Keep the camera on the storm/water briefly; avoid dramatic victim imagery.

## Ending D — Time expired

> 灯塔：风墙封港。线路暂时关了。

The harbor fades colder, but the run can restart immediately.

## Narrative rules

- one short line at a time;
- no exposition dump;
- never block controls;
- dialogue should often point toward a real gameplay state;
- ending tone reflects rescue count;
- no copied Ink/Ink-Library story text; Ink is only an authoring-structure reference.
